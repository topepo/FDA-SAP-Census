# Clobazam (Onfi) — NDA 202067

Part of the [numeric rank tests deep dive](README.md). Role of the rank test: rank test specified or run, but not the operative primary analysis.

**Source documents:** [FDA Statistical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) · [FDA Medical/Clinical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) · [FDA Summary Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000SumR.pdf) · [Label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2011/202067s000lbl.pdf)

Census entry: [`README.md`](../../README.md) line 22254. Epilepsy (Lennox-Gastaut syndrome, adjunctive). Lundbeck (Ovation). Approved 2011.

**Summary:** The rank test was NOT the primary analysis of the pivotal trial. OV-1012 (placebo and three doses) used a parametric ANCOVA on percent reduction in weekly drop-seizure rate (treatment, pooled center, baseline rate) with a step-down across doses at p ≤ 0.01; a rank ANCOVA (sensitivity analysis K) and a post-unblinding Wilcoxon rank-sum test were supportive. The supportive phase 2 study OV-1002 (high vs low dose, no placebo) used a 1-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test of within-group change from baseline for each dose group as its primary analysis, and a 1-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test for high vs low dose as a supportive analysis.

**Census discrepancies** (errors found in this submission's entry in [`../../README.md`](../../README.md)):

- The census: "OV-1002 used a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing high-dose to low-dose directly". This copies an internal inconsistency in the FDA Statistical Review, whose text says "1-sided Wilcoxon signed rank test ... to compare the high-dose to the low-dose group" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.3-4, p.8] while its own Table 4 footnote says "1-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.11]. The FDA Clinical Review resolves it: the signed-rank test was within each dose group (change from baseline), and the between-group comparison was a 1-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test, labeled supportive [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.31].
- The census lists the rank ANCOVA (K) among "pre-specified" sensitivity analyses; that matches MedR p.18-19 for drop seizures (only the linear trend test, the Wilcoxon rank-sum test and analysis L are named as added after unblinding). For total and non-drop seizures the rank-transformed analysis was added after unblinding [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.19].
- The census does not report the rank-based results: K p-values 0.0274 / 0.0004 / <0.0001 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 5 p.13].
- The census gives OV-1012 results as medians with ANCOVA p-values without noting that the ANCOVA compares means (LS means), so the table mixes summaries.

No SAP or protocol is public. Sources are the FDA statistical, clinical and summary reviews and the label.

## OV-1012 (pivotal; parametric primary with rank sensitivity)

### Design

- Placebo, low (0.25 mg/kg, max 10 mg/day), medium (0.5 mg/kg, max 20 mg/day), high (1.0 mg/kg, max 40 mg/day); randomized 59 / 58 / 62 / 59 (N = 238) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9]. Allocation ratio: Not stated (approximately equal).
- Stratification: weight group, 12.5 to ≤30 kg vs >30 kg (2 levels) [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.15].
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled; 4-week baseline, 3-week titration, 12-week maintenance.
- Primary population (MITT): randomized, received at least 1 dose, had baseline data, and at least 1 daily seizure measurement in the maintenance period [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.18]. N = 57 / 53 / 58 / 49 (217 total) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 4 p.11].

### Endpoint

- Percent reduction in average weekly rate of drop seizures from the 4-week baseline to the 12-week maintenance period; positive = reduction [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7].
- Derivation: weekly rate = (number of drop seizures in period / number of days recorded in period) x 7 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7]. Percent reduction formula: Not stated explicitly.
- Diary check boxes "10-20" and ">20" needed numeric conventions (10 and 20 implied as the primary convention; 20/30 and 20/50 in sensitivity analyses C-G) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Distribution: "highly non-normal distribution of the data" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.20]. Bounded above at 100, long lower tail (placebo range -374 to 100; mean 12.5, SD 72.7, median 23.2) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 4].

### Hypothesis and contrast

- Each dose vs placebo, two-sided pairwise comparisons within the ANCOVA [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 4 footnote 2].
- Criterion: "Superiority of clobazam to placebo (p ≤ 0.01) was to be considered a statistical evidence in a single multicenter study" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7]; SumR: "Each comparison to placebo was to be tested at alpha=1%, not the traditional 5%." [[SumR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000SumR.pdf) p.3]

### Decision rule

None. The ANCOVA was primary regardless of distribution; no normality test triggered a switch. The Wilcoxon rank-sum analysis was "added after breaking the blind because of the highly non-normal distribution of the data" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.20].

### Primary analysis method (parametric)

ANCOVA, dependent variable percent reduction in drop seizures [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7; [MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.18]:

| Term | Type | Levels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treatment | categorical | 4 | placebo, low, medium, high |
| Pooled center | categorical | Not stated | centers with <16 randomized pooled geographically (smallest with next smallest in region) until ≥16 per pooled center [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.19] |
| Baseline drop seizure rate | continuous | - | weekly rate |

- Statistic: pairwise treatment contrasts vs placebo from the model; LS means; "Ninety-five percent confidence intervals (CIs) were provided for the mean difference" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.18].

### Rank sensitivity analysis (K)

- "K: Using rank of percent reduction as the response variable with an ANCOVA model including treatment, pooled center, and baseline seizure rate as effects." [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8]
- What is ranked: the response only (percent reduction). Baseline seizure rate enters unranked as described. Ranking scope (overall vs within center), tie handling: Not stated.

| Term | Type | Levels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Response: percent reduction | ranked | - | scope Not stated |
| Treatment | categorical | 4 | |
| Pooled center | categorical | Not stated | |
| Baseline seizure rate | continuous (not stated as ranked) | - | |

- Post-unblinding addition: "a 2-sided, pairwise comparison of each clobazam dose group versus placebo performed using the Wilcoxon rank-sum test (for median change from baseline)" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.18]. Exact vs asymptotic: Not stated.

### Effect estimate and interval

- LS mean percent reduction per arm and mean differences with 95% CI (values in MedR Table 7, an image). Medians reported descriptively. No Hodges-Lehmann estimate for OV-1012 found.

### Missing data and intercurrent events

- "No explicit imputation of missing data was made"; weekly rate uses days with non-missing diary data in the maintenance period [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.4]. Reviewer: this "is not different from the last observation carried forward (LOCF) approach" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.4].
- Completion: 69.5% / 86.2% / 72.6% / 69.5% [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 2 p.9].
- Sensitivity I: impute baseline rate for the remainder of maintenance for adverse-event discontinuations.

### Multiplicity

Step-down: high vs placebo first; if significant, medium vs placebo; if significant, low vs placebo [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.18].

### Sample size assumptions

Not stated in the reviews consulted.

### Software and code

Not stated (reviewer used SAS datasets). No code.

## OV-1002 (supportive phase 2; rank tests)

### Design

- Low dose (0.25 mg/kg, max 10 mg/day) vs high dose (1.0 mg/kg, max 40 mg/day); no placebo; randomized 32 / 36 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9].
- Stratification: 6 weight categories (12.5-17.5, 17.6-22.5, 22.6-27.5, 27.6-32.5, 32.6-37.5, >37.6 kg) [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.29].
- Double-blind; 4-week baseline, 3-week titration, 4-week maintenance.
- MITT: randomized, received study drug, baseline and post-baseline measurement, and at least 1 measurement in maintenance; N = 29 / 32 [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.31; [StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 4].

### Endpoint

Percent reduction in drop seizures (average per week) from the 4-week baseline to the 4-week maintenance period [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.30]. For non-completers, calculated from the start of maintenance to withdrawal [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.31].

### Hypothesis and contrast

- Primary: within each dose group, change from baseline; one-sided. Alpha: Not stated.
- Supportive: high vs low dose; one-sided.

### Primary analysis method

- "The 1-sided Wilcoxon signed rank test was used to assess the difference from baseline for each dose group. As a supportive efficacy analysis, the 1-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test was used to compare the high-dose to the low-dose group." [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.31]
- Signed-rank test: what is ranked is the absolute within-subject change (baseline vs maintenance), per dose group. Whether applied to the percent reduction or to the raw rate difference, zero/tie handling, exact vs asymptotic: Not stated.
- Rank-sum test: percent reduction ranked over both groups; unstratified as described; exact vs asymptotic: Not stated.

### Effect estimate and interval

"The difference between the treatment group medians was 44.7% and was statistically significantly in favor of the high-dose group (p < 0.0001,95% CI 21.7% to 66.7%)." [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.32]. Estimator and CI method: Not stated.

### Missing data

No imputation; rate over available maintenance days. Completion 87.5% / 83.3% [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 2].

## Implementation notes (inference)

- Inference: analysis K is a response-only rank transform: `PROC RANK` on percent reduction over all MITT subjects, then `PROC GLM; CLASS trt pcenter; MODEL r_pctred = trt pcenter base;` with pairwise contrasts vs placebo. Unlike the perampanel and ezogabine rank ANCOVAs, the covariate is not described as ranked.
- Inference: the OV-1002 "difference between the treatment group medians" of 44.7% with CI 21.7 to 66.7 does not equal the difference of the reported medians (93 - 29 or 93 - 41), so it is probably a Hodges-Lehmann shift estimate with its distribution-free CI. Not stated in the source.
- A within-group signed-rank test without a control arm is a weak primary analysis (no control for placebo response or regression to the mean); FDA relied on the high-vs-low rank-sum comparison ("demonstrated efficacy by showing superiority of the high dose over the low dose" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2011/202067Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.33]).
- For a new analysis: `wilcox.test(pct_red ~ dose, alternative = "greater", conf.int = TRUE)` for the rank-sum; `wilcox.test(pct_red, mu = 0, alternative = "greater")` per group for the signed-rank on percent reduction (assumed scale).
