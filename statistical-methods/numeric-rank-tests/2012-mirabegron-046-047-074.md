# Mirabegron (Myrbetriq) — NDA 202611

Part of the [numeric rank tests deep dive](README.md). Role of the rank test: pre-specified primary analysis.

**Source documents:** [FDA Statistical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) · [Label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2012/202611s000lbl.pdf) · [FDA Medical/Clinical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000MedR.pdf) · [FDA Summary Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000SumR.pdf)

Census entry: [`README.md`](../../README.md) line 36685. Urology (overactive bladder). Astellas Pharma Global Development. Approved 2012.

**Summary:** In three 12-week placebo-controlled trials (178-CL-046, -047, -074) the co-primary endpoint "change from baseline to final visit in mean number of incontinence episodes per 24 hours" was tested, for each active dose versus placebo, with a "stratified rank ANCOVA": region-wise standardized ranks of change and of baseline, a within-region regression of ranked change on ranked baseline and gender, and a stratified mean score test on the residuals with region as stratum. The p-value comes from that rank procedure, but the reported effect (adjusted mean change, difference, 95% CI) comes from an ordinary ANCOVA (treatment, gender, region, baseline). All mirabegron doses were significant in all three trials. The other co-primary endpoint (micturitions) used the ordinary ANCOVA for both estimate and p-value.

**Census discrepancies** (errors found in this submission's entry in [`../../README.md`](../../README.md)):

- The census describes the method as "region-standardized ranks, gender and baseline rank as covariates", which is right but omits two steps that matter for coding: the regression is fitted separately within each region, and the test is a stratified mean score test on the residuals (not an F-test from a single ANCOVA on ranks).
- The census does not say that the ranking is redone for each pairwise comparison (ranks "across the 2 treatment groups").
- The census does not say that the effect estimates and CIs shown next to the rank p-values are from the parametric ANCOVA.
- The census says "LOCF"; correct, but Week 4 key secondary endpoints used observed data.

## Studies 178-CL-046, 178-CL-047, 178-CL-074 (same method in all three)

### Design

- 046: placebo 497, mirabegron 50 mg 497, mirabegron 100 mg 498, tolterodine SR 4 mg 495; 1:1:1:1. 047: placebo 454, 50 mg 442, 100 mg 433; 1:1:1. 074: placebo 433, 25 mg 433, 50 mg 440; 1:1:1 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7-8, Table 1].
- Randomization stratification factors: Not stated.
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled (046 also active-controlled), 2-week single-blind placebo run-in, 12-week treatment [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Analysis population for the incontinence endpoint: FAS-I (FAS incontinence) = randomized, took at least one dose of double-blind drug, had a micturition measurement and at least one incontinence episode in the baseline diary, and at least one post-baseline diary with a micturition measurement [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.11]. FAS-I N: 046: 291 / 293 / 281 / 300 (placebo / 50 / 100 / tolterodine); 047: 325 / 312 / 296; 074: 262 / 254 / 257 (placebo / 25 / 50) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.11-12, Tables 5-7]. FAS-I was 58.6%, 70.2%, 59.2% of randomized subjects.

### Endpoint

- Change from baseline to end of treatment (final visit) in mean number of incontinence episodes per 24 hours, from a 3-day micturition diary completed before each visit [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8-9].
- Derivation: mean per 24 hours over the diary days (exact averaging rule Not stated). Final visit = Week 12 value, or the last available earlier post-baseline diary average within a designated visit window and post-dosing window [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.13].
- Variable type: non-negative average count (multiples of 1/3 for a 3-day diary; inference). Sources say only that change from baseline was non-normally distributed [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.5, p.12]. Median baseline in incontinent patients was 2.0/day in 047 [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000MedR.pdf), Study 047 reviewer summary]. Baseline means 2.4-3.0 [[Label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2012/202611s000lbl.pdf) Table 3].

### Hypothesis and contrast

- Each mirabegron dose vs placebo (and tolterodine vs placebo in 046, outside multiplicity control); two-sided; overall alpha 0.05 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9].
- Null/alternative: Not stated. Estimand: Not stated (2012 review).

### Decision rule (only if the method was contingent)

- The review words the choice as conditional: "If the normality assumption for the data of the efficacy endpoint was not violated, the pair-wise P-values were derived from the above ANCOVA model" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.12]. ### Primary analysis method

- Test name as given: "stratified rank ANCOVA" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.12-13].
- What is ranked: both the response (change from baseline) and the covariate (baseline value). Scope: within each geographical region, across the 2 treatment groups in the pairwise comparison (so ranks are recomputed for each active-vs-placebo pair, pooling the two arms). Score type: "standardized ranks" (formula Not stated). Tie handling: Not stated.
- Stratification: geographical region. Applicant's levels: 046: Eastern Europe, Western Europe (2); 047: Northeast US, Midwest US, South US, East US and Central US, and Canada (as listed in the review; 5 or 6 levels, the wording is ambiguous); 074: Eastern Europe, Western Europe, Canada, Northeastern US, Midwestern US, Southern US, Western US (7) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.13]. Strata combined by a "stratified mean score test" on residual scores.
- Model terms for the per-region regression (step 2):

| Term | Type | Levels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standardized rank of change from baseline | ranked (response) | — | ranks within region across the 2 arms |
| Standardized rank of baseline | ranked covariate | — | ranks within region across the 2 arms |
| Gender | categorical covariate | 2 | |
| Treatment | — | — | NOT in the regression; enters only in step 3 as the row variable of the mean score test |
| Region | stratum | 2-7 | regression fitted separately per region; also the stratum of the test |

- Test statistic and reference distribution: stratified mean score test comparing the two groups using residuals as scores and region as stratum; reference distribution Not stated (chi-square with 1 df is the standard form; see Implementation notes). Continuity correction: Not stated.
- Parametric model that accompanied it (source of the estimates):

| Term | Type | Levels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Change from baseline | continuous (response) | — | |
| Treatment group | categorical | 3 or 4 | all arms of the study in one model (inference from "each mirabegron treatment group and placebo") |
| Gender | categorical | 2 | |
| Geographical region | categorical | 2-7 | |
| Baseline measurement | continuous | — | |

### Effect estimate and interval

- Adjusted (LS) mean change per arm and LS mean difference vs placebo with two-sided 95% CI, all from the parametric ANCOVA above, even for the incontinence endpoint [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.12; table footnotes p.14-16; [Label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2012/202611s000lbl.pdf) Table 3 footnote "Least squares mean adjusted for baseline, gender, and geographical region"]. No rank-based estimator (no Hodges-Lehmann, no medians) is presented.

### Missing data and intercurrent events

- LOCF for final visit: last available post-baseline diary average within the visit/post-dosing window. Week 4 endpoints: observed data [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9, p.13 Table 8].
- Subjects without any post-baseline diary are excluded by the FAS definition. FAS was 95.2-96.8% of randomized per arm [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Tables 5-7].
- Study discontinuation 9.9% (046), 13.6% (047), 12.7% (074) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.10].

### Multiplicity

- Stepwise parallel gatekeeping at 0.05: Stage 1 incontinence episodes at final visit; Stage 2 micturitions at final visit; Stage 3 volume voided; Stage 4 incontinence at Week 4; Stage 5 micturitions at Week 4 (Stages 6-8 in 074 only). A dose proceeds only if significant at all earlier stages. Within a stage, Hochberg across the two mirabegron doses (largest p ≤ 0.05 → both significant; otherwise smaller p compared with 0.025). If only one dose enters a stage it is tested at 0.025 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9-10].
- Tolterodine vs placebo: no adjustment [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202611Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9].

### Implementation notes (inference)

- Inference: the three steps are the stratified nonparametric (rank) ANCOVA of Koch/Stokes et al. ("Categorical Data Analysis Using the SAS System", rank analysis of covariance). A SAS sketch that matches the description: subset to one active arm plus placebo; `PROC RANK NPLUS1 TIES=MEAN; BY region; VAR change baseline;` (standardized rank = rank/(n_region+1)); `PROC REG; BY region; MODEL r_change = r_baseline gender; OUTPUT R=resid;`; `PROC FREQ; TABLES region*trt*resid / NOPRINT CMH2 SCORES=TABLE;` and read the "Row Mean Scores Differ" statistic (1 df chi-square for two groups).
- In R: within-region `rank(x)/(n+1)`, `lm()` by region, then the stratified mean score (extended Mantel-Haenszel) statistic on residuals, e.g. `coin::independence_test(resid ~ trt | region, teststat = "quadratic")` or a hand-coded sum over strata of (observed − expected residual sum in the active arm) divided by the square root of the summed hypergeometric variances.
- Because the residuals are computed without treatment in the model, the test is of the null of no treatment difference after covariate adjustment; it yields no effect estimate, which is why the applicant reported parametric LS means.
- A programmer has to decide: the standardized rank divisor (n+1 vs n); tie method (midranks assumed); how gender enters when a region has one gender only; whether small regions are pooled (047 lists a combined "East US and Central US"); asymptotic vs exact reference distribution.
- The CI and the p-value come from different models and need not agree (e.g. 047, 50 mg: CI excludes 0 with p=0.026; compatible here).
