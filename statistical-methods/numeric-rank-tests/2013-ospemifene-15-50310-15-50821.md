# Ospemifene (Osphena) — NDA 203505

Part of the [numeric rank tests deep dive](README.md). Role of the rank test: parametric primary with a rank-based fallback that was invoked.

**Source documents:** [FDA Statistical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) · [FDA Medical/Clinical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) · [Label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/203505s000lbl.pdf)

Census entry: [`README.md`](../../README.md) line 36839. Women's health (dyspareunia due to vulvovaginal atrophy). Shionogi. Approved 2013.

**Summary:** The planned analysis of the three numeric co-primary endpoints (change to Week 12 in % superficial cells, % parabasal cells, vaginal pH) was a parametric ANCOVA, with a rank-based fallback if ANCOVA assumptions were "severely violated". The fallback WAS invoked in the sponsor's original clinical study reports (ITT population): for all three numeric endpoints in Study 15-50310 and for % superficial cells only in Study 15-50821. However, the analyses that FDA treated as primary (mITT population, sponsor addenda of July 2012 and the FDA statistical reviewer's own analysis) and that support the label used the parametric ANCOVA for all three endpoints. The sources give almost no mechanics for the rank method.

**Census discrepancies** (errors found in this submission's entry in [`../../README.md`](../../README.md)):

- The census says only "with a rank-based ANCOVA fallback if model assumptions were violated" and does not say whether it was used. It was used in the original CSR analyses (see above), but not in the mITT analyses behind the label.
- The census lists the ANCOVA terms as applying to "the three continuous/cytologic co-primary endpoints" in both studies; uterine status was a term only in 15-50310.
- The fourth co-primary endpoint (most bothersome symptom severity, 0-3 ordinal) was analyzed by a CMH row mean scores test, which is itself a rank-type/score test; census labels it only "Cochran-Mantel-Haenszel test". It is categorical, so it is outside this case's scope, but note that the label presents it as "LS Mean Change from Baseline (SE)" with the CMH p-value.

## Study 15-50310

### Design

- Ospemifene 30 mg 282, ospemifene 60 mg 276, placebo 268 (826 randomized; planned 795, 265/arm); 76 US sites [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8, Table 3 p.11].
- Randomization stratified by uterine status (intact or hysterectomized; 2 levels) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, 12 weeks; all subjects received non-hormonal lubricant as needed [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Original CSR primary population: ITT = randomized and received at least 1 dose (cells N 274/272/261; pH N 282/276/268) [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) Table 11, p.75]. FDA-preferred population: mITT = ITT subjects with vaginal pH >5, superficial cells ≤5%, and a most bothersome moderate-to-severe symptom of dryness or dyspareunia (FDA mITT N 219/223/213; sponsor's mITT 257/254/247) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9, Table 3].

### Endpoint

- Change from baseline (screening) to Week 12 in (a) percentage of parabasal cells and (b) percentage of superficial cells in the maturation index of the vaginal smear, and (c) vaginal pH [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Variable type and distribution: percentages bounded 0-100 with strong floor/ceiling effects implied by the summaries (baseline % superficial mean 0.7-1.25 with SD 1.3-3.4 by eligibility design; % parabasal mean about 40 with SD about 38) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 4; [MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) Table 11]. The sources say only that "the ANCOVA assumptions were severely violated in Study 15-50310" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.46].

### Hypothesis and contrast

- Each active dose vs placebo; two-sided alpha 5% [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.10]. Null/alternative: Not stated. Estimand: Not stated.

### Decision rule

- Trigger: "In case of the ANCOVA assumptions were severely violated" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9]. Which assumptions, which diagnostic or test, what threshold, applied to which residuals, and who decided: Not stated.
- Outcome: invoked for all three numeric co-primary endpoints in the original CSR (ITT) analysis [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.46; Table 11 footnote a, p.76]. Not invoked in the July 2012 addendum (mITT) analysis, where the same three endpoints were analyzed by ANCOVA [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) Table 12 footnote b, p.78], nor by the FDA statistical reviewer [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 5 footnote b, p.13].

### Primary analysis method

- Test name as given: "rank-based ANCOVA ... including study center and uterus status" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9]; "a nonparametric approach (rank-based analysis of variance method) ... stratifying by study center and by uterine status separately" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.46]; table footnote: "rank-based analysis of variance stratified by uterine status and pooled center" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.76].
- What is ranked, ranking scope, score type, tie handling: Not stated.
- Stratification: uterine status (2 levels) and pooled center (number of pooled centers Not stated; small centers pooled by geographical location [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.45]). How strata were combined: Not stated. The phrase "separately" in the medical review is ambiguous (see Implementation notes).
- Whether baseline was a covariate in the rank analysis: the statistical review calls it a "rank-based ANCOVA"; the medical review and its table footnote call it "analysis of variance". Not resolved by the sources.
- Test statistic and reference distribution: Not stated.
- Parametric model it replaced (and which was used for mITT):

### Effect estimate and interval

- Rank analysis (ITT, CSR): no model-based estimate is shown; the table gives raw baseline mean (SD), Week 12 mean (SD), mean change (SD) and the p-value only [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf) Table 11].
- ANCOVA (mITT): LS mean (SE) per arm; FDA reviewer adds difference and 95% CI [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 5].

### Missing data and intercurrent events

- LOCF; with no post-treatment value the baseline value was carried forward; if baseline was missing but a post-baseline value existed the change score was set to zero [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9].
- Discontinuation 16.6% overall (20.2% / 15.2% / 14.2%) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 3].

### Multiplicity

- Step-down across doses: 60 mg first; 30 mg tested only if all four co-primary endpoints significant for 60 mg. No adjustment between the two symptoms (agreed with FDA); the reviewer applied Bonferroni between symptoms, testing at two-sided 0.0125 in 15-50310 and 0.025 in 15-50821 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.9-10, p.15].

### Sample size assumptions

- Parabasal cells: 20% difference, SD 40.3; superficial cells: 5% difference, SD 12.4; pH: 0.5 difference, SD 1.1; symptom severity: 81% power for 0.4 difference, SD 0.94; 40% with dryness as most bothersome symptom; two-sided alpha 5%; 15% dropout [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.10]. These are all parametric (mean/SD) assumptions.

## Study 15-50821

### Design

- Ospemifene 60 mg 463, placebo 456 (919 randomized; planned 750); 112 US sites [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8, Table 3].
- Randomization stratified by most bothersome symptom (vaginal dryness or dyspareunia; 2 strata), and each stratum was analyzed separately [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8; [MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/203505Orig1s000MedR.pdf)].

## Implementation notes (inference)

- Inference: the wording ("rank-based analysis of variance stratified by uterine status and pooled center", "rank-based ANCOVA, stratifying by study center") is consistent with either (a) a van Elteren / CMH row-mean-scores test on within-stratum ranks (e.g. `PROC FREQ ... CMH2 SCORES=MODRIDIT` with strata center × uterine status), or (b) the Koch/Stokes rank ANCOVA (within-stratum ranks of change and baseline, residuals from regression of ranked change on ranked baseline, then a stratified mean score test), or (c) an ordinary ANOVA/ANCOVA on rank-transformed data with center and uterine status as factors. The sources do not allow choosing among these. The sponsor used a CMH row mean scores test for the ordinal endpoint in the same studies, which makes (a) or (b) plausible.
- "Stratifying by study center and by uterine status separately" could mean two separate stratified analyses or strata formed by the two factors; the table footnote ("stratified by uterine status and pooled center") suggests one analysis with both.
- Comparisons were pairwise (each active vs placebo), so in 15-50310 a programmer must decide whether to rank across all three arms or within each pair.
- With eligibility requiring ≤5% superficial cells at baseline, the baseline covariate is nearly degenerate and the change is heavily tied at 0; midranks and an asymptotic stratified test are the natural choices, but none is documented.
- No effect estimate accompanied the rank p-values; if one is wanted, a stratified Hodges-Lehmann estimate would have to be added by the programmer.
