# Perampanel (Fycompa) — NDA 202834

Part of the [numeric rank tests deep dive](README.md). Role of the rank test: pre-specified primary analysis.

**Source documents:** [FDA Statistical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) · [FDA Medical/Clinical Review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) · [Label](http://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2012/202834lbl.pdf)

Census entry: [`README.md`](../../README.md) line 22482. Epilepsy (partial-onset seizures, adjunctive). Eisai. Approved 2012.

**Summary:** Three placebo-controlled trials (304, 305: 8 and 12 mg; 306: 2, 4 and 8 mg) compared each dose with placebo on percent change from baseline in seizure frequency per 28 days. The primary analysis was a rank ANCOVA: percent change and baseline seizure frequency were ranked separately, then ANCOVA was fitted to the ranked percent change with treatment and pooled country as factors and ranked baseline as covariate. The effect size was reported separately as a Hodges-Lehmann median difference with 95% CI. All doses except 2 mg were significant in the Full ITT set; Study 304's 8 mg comparison was not significant (p = 0.0812) in the originally planned ITT set.

**Census discrepancies** (errors found in this submission's entry in [`../../README.md`](../../README.md)):

- Method description is correct.
- The census says the primary endpoint is percent change "during the maintenance period ... (full ITT population, LOCF)". The FDA results tables and the label report the Double-blind Phase (19-week titration plus maintenance) as the primary result [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Tables 5-7 p.12; [Label](http://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2012/202834lbl.pdf) §14]. The StatR says the Maintenance Period was "originally planned for all three studies, and The Double Blind Period amended later for Study 305" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7].
- Multiplicity: the StatR text says 304/305 start with 8 mg then 12 mg, and 306 starts with 8 mg, then 4 mg, then 2 mg. The census matches this. The Clinical Review's one-line summary ("first test a lower dose ... then the next higher dose") is looser [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.93].
- The census gives no numeric results; they are tabulated below (recovered by OCR from image tables).
- Minor: the StatR text gives p = 0.0184 for 304 12 mg while its Table 5 and the label give 0.0158.

## Studies 304, 305, 306 (E2007-G000-304/305/306) — same method

### Design

- 304: placebo, 8 mg, 12 mg; randomized 390; Full ITT 121 / 133 / 133. 305: placebo, 8 mg, 12 mg; randomized 389; Full ITT 136 / 129 / 121. 306: placebo, 2 mg, 4 mg, 8 mg; randomized 712; Full ITT 184 / 180 / 172 / 169 [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 1 p.4, Tables 5-7 p.12].
- Allocation: "randomly assigned with equal probability" [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.85]. Randomization stratification factors: Not stated.
- Double-blind, placebo-controlled; 6-week pre-randomization baseline, 6-week titration, 13-week maintenance.
- Analysis sets [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.68]:
    - Full ITT (final primary): all randomized who received study drug and had any seizure frequency data from the Double-blind Phase.
    - ITT (originally planned primary): additionally at least 2 weeks of seizure data in both the Prerandomization and Double-blind Phases.

### Endpoint

- Percent change in seizure frequency per 28 days during treatment relative to the Prerandomization Phase.
- Derivation: seizure frequency per 28 days "calculated as (the number of seizures over the time interval multiplied by 28) and divided by the number of days in the interval" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.7].
- Distribution: baseline frequency strongly right-skewed (306 4 mg arm: mean 62.56, SD 354.87, median 10.02, max 4503.9) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 4, OCR p.11]. No explicit normality statement in the sources.

### Hypothesis and contrast

- Each dose vs placebo, two-sided alpha 0.05, in a fixed order [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Dose-response trend: "a linear contrast using the ranked ANCOVA" (secondary) [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].

### Decision rule

Not contingent. Rank ANCOVA was pre-specified; log-transformation ANCOVA was a pre-specified robustness check.

### Primary analysis method

- Test name: rank ANCOVA ("ranked ANCOVA").
- What is ranked: (1) percent change per 28 days during treatment, (2) baseline seizure frequency per 28 days; "rank transformed separately". Ranking scope: Not stated (no mention of ranking within country, so presumably over all subjects in the analysis set). Tie handling and score type: Not stated.
- Stratification: pooled country enters as a model factor, not as strata for the ranking (as described).
- Test statistic and reference distribution: Not stated (pairwise treatment contrasts within the ANCOVA, presumably F or t).

| Term | Type | Levels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Response: percent change in seizure frequency per 28 days | ranked | - | ranked separately from baseline |
| Treatment | categorical | 3 (304, 305); 4 (306) | pairwise vs placebo; linear contrast for trend |
| Pooled country | categorical | Not stated | pooling rule below |
| Baseline seizure frequency per 28 days | ranked | - | covariate |

- Country pooling: centers pooled within country; each country needs at least 12 subjects; countries with <12 are sorted by size and pooled starting from the smallest until 12 is reached [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8-9].
- Robustness model: "Log-transformation based ANCOVA" [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8]. Exact transform and terms: Not stated (the clinical review says the same factors: treatment, pooled country, prerandomization seizure frequency as covariate for the 306 description [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.72]).
- Pooled-studies exploratory model: ranked percent change; ranked baseline as covariate; treatment, region, treatment-by-region as factors [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.119].

### Effect estimate and interval

- "Hodges−Lehmann estimator and 95% confidence interval (CI) for the estimator were calculated." [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8]. The sponsor table footnote (OCR text, so not quoted verbatim) says the median difference to placebo and its 95% CI are based on the Hodges-Lehmann method, and that the p-value is from the rank ANCOVA with pre- and post-randomization measurements ranked separately [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) Table 15, medr_ocr_p328.txt, p.94].
- The HL estimate is unadjusted (two-sample, each dose vs placebo); it does not come from the ANCOVA. Stratified HL: not indicated.

### Missing data and intercurrent events

- "LOCF" here is a seizure-rate rule, not visit carry-forward: if the Maintenance Period lasted less than 8 weeks, the last 8 weeks of diary data in the treatment phase (titration plus maintenance) are used for Maintenance-LOCF [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Subjects with less than 2 weeks of Double-blind seizure data: if more than 10% of randomized subjects are affected, use their last 2 weeks of data (including pre-randomization days); otherwise set to missing [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8].
- Six early dropouts (1 to 13 days on treatment) excluded from the ITT set but included in Full ITT in Study 304 had LOCF values from -100% to 43.66% [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) Table 17 p.17].

### Multiplicity

Closed sequential procedure at two-sided 0.05 per step. 304/305: 8 mg vs placebo, then 12 mg. 306: 8 mg, then 4 mg, then 2 mg [[StatR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000StatR.pdf) p.8; [MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.95].

### Sample size assumptions

Not stated in the reviews (planned N: 305, 375; 306, 680 [[MedR](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/202834Orig1s000MedR.pdf) p.69, 71]).

Not stated. No code.

### Implementation notes (inference)

- Inference: this is the Conover-Iman rank-transform ANCOVA. `PROC RANK` (default mean ranks for ties) on percent change and on baseline over the whole analysis set, then `PROC GLM` or `PROC MIXED`: `CLASS trt pcountry; MODEL r_pchg = trt pcountry r_base;` with `LSMEANS trt / PDIFF` or `ESTIMATE` statements for each dose vs placebo and a `CONTRAST` with linear coefficients (e.g. -3 -1 1 3 for four equally spaced groups, or dose-proportional coefficients; the sources do not say which) for trend.
- Open decision: whether each pairwise p-value comes from the full multi-arm model (pooled error) or from two-arm subsets with re-ranking. The wording ("with treatment ... as factors") suggests one model per study.
- The HL estimate and CI are computed separately per dose vs placebo on the raw percent changes (`PROC NPAR1WAY ... HL` or R `wilcox.test(conf.int = TRUE)`), so the p-value (covariate- and country-adjusted, on ranks) and the estimate (unadjusted) come from different procedures. The CIs are asymmetric about the estimate (e.g. -13.53: -26.17, -1.94), consistent with the distribution-free (Moses) interval rather than a Wald interval.
- R sketch: `d$ry <- rank(d$pchg); d$rx <- rank(d$base); fit <- lm(ry ~ trt + pcountry + rx, d); emmeans::contrast(emmeans(fit, "trt"), "trt.vs.ctrl")`.
